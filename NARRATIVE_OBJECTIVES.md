# Movie Vibe — Phase 1: Narrative & Objectives

## 1. Executive Summary & Vision
**Movie Vibe** is an emotion-driven, community-infused web application designed for film enthusiasts and casual viewers. Moving beyond traditional genre-based navigation, Movie Vibe offers a sensory discovery platform where users can browse films based on their current **mood/vibe**, filter by **language preferences**, explore community scene reviews with famous dialogues, and maintain personal watchlists.

---

## 2. Core Feature Specifications

| Feature | Category | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mood & Vibe Discovery** | Discovery Engine | Select current emotion/vibe to instantly receive tailored movie recommendations. |
| **Language Selection** | Filtering System | Filter recommendations across preferred regional and international languages. |
| **"Vibe Check" Community Reviews** | Social & Review | Users post movie stills/photos paired with famous dialogues, personal thoughts, and a 5-star "Vibe Check" rating. |
| **Community Engagement** | Interaction | Like and interact with other users' photo & dialogue reviews. |
| **Personal Watchlist** | Curation | Save movie posters directly to a personal watchlist for future viewing. |
| **"Top 5 Daily Picks"** | Fallback Engine | Curated top 5 movie recommendations for users experiencing decision fatigue or operating without a specific mood. |
